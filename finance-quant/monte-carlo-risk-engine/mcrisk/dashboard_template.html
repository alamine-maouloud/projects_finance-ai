    Model validation

    Every engine is checked against a closed form

    Each Monte Carlo estimate is compared with an exact or semi-analytic reference. The z-score is the gap in units of the estimate's own standard error, so values within ±2 are what an unbiased estimator produces 95% of the time.

      Performance

      Throughput of the credit engine

      The NumPy engine draws one latent variable per obligor and scenario. The C++ kernel samples defaults by exact Bernoulli thinning: a bound on each block's conditional PD, geometric skipping, then acceptance. Its cost grows with the number of defaults, not the number of obligors.

      Million obligor-scenarios per second

      Plain Monte Carlo, Gaussian copula, 10 threads (Apple M4). Higher is better.

      Methods

      Models and estimators

      Credit: multi-factor copula

      X_i = b_i·F + √(1−R_i) ε_i,   F ~ N(0, Σ_F)
default_i ⇔ X_i < Φ⁻¹(PD_i)
t-copula: X_i · √(ν/W),  W ~ χ²_ν

      Beta LGD correlated with the obligor's systematic factor gives downturn severity. Stress tests condition a subset of factors and draw the rest from N(Σ_RS Σ_SS⁻¹ y, Σ_RR − Σ_RS Σ_SS⁻¹ Σ_SR).

      Importance sampling

      Z ~ N(μ, I),  μ = argmax  −θ(z)x + ψ(θ(z), z) − |z|²/2
q_i = p_i e^{θc_i} / (1 − p_i + p_i e^{θc_i}),  ψ'(θ) = x
w = f / (λf + (1−λ)h)     (defensive mixture)

      Glasserman & Li (2005) mean shift and exponential twisting; θ solved row-wise by Newton on log ψ′. A 10% defensive share bounds the weights by 10 so the body of the distribution stays exact.

      Risk measures and allocation

      VaR_α = inf{x : P(L > x) ≤ 1 − α}
ES_α  = VaR_α + E[(L − VaR_α)⁺] / (1 − α)
RC_i  = E[L_i | L ≥ VaR_α]     Σ RC_i = E[L | L ≥ VaR_α]

      Weighted estimators serve plain MC and IS alike; standard errors by batch means. Euler contributions replay each chunk's random stream exactly in a second pass.

      FRTB Expected Shortfall

      ES = √( ES_T(P)² + Σ_{j≥2} (ES_T(P,j) √((LH_j − LH_{j−1})/T))² )
IMCC = ½ ES_total + ½ Σ_classes ES_class

      Filtered historical simulation: GARCH(1,1) by QMLE with variance targeting, bootstrap of standardised residual vectors, volatility propagated path-wise over 10 days. Backtests: Kupiec POF, Christoffersen independence, Basel traffic light.

      Hull-White and exposure

      r = x + φ(t),  dx = −a x dt + σ dW
P(t,T) = P(0,T)/P(0,t) · exp(½[V(t,T) − V(0,T) + V(0,t)] − B(t,T)x)
D(0,t) = P(0,t) · exp(−∫x − ½V(0,t))

      (x, ∫x) is simulated jointly and exactly. Collateral at t reflects V(t − MPoR) net of thresholds; CVA = (1−R) Σ E[D·E⁺] ΔPD, with wrong-way risk through a calibrated stochastic intensity (Hull & White, 2012).

      Pricing library

      Heston: QE scheme (Andersen 2008) + martingale correction
        vs Gil-Pelaez inversion ("little trap" CF)
Asian:  Sobol + Brownian bridge + geometric control variate
American: Longstaff-Schwartz, out-of-sample exercise rule

      The same variance-reduction toolkit (antithetic, control variates, randomised QMC) is used across engines and validated against closed forms.
